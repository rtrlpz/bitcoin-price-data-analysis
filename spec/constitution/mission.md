# Mission

## One-Line Purpose
A professional-grade, dark-mode multi-asset quantitative trading dashboard that democratizes institutional-quality market analysis, paper trading, and risk management for self-directed retail traders.

## Target Users
- **Primary**: Beginner-to-intermediate retail traders with $500–$50,000 capital
- **Secondary**: Crypto investors seeking structured technical analysis
- **Not for**: Institutional desks, high-frequency traders, options specialists

## Core Problems Solved
1. **Overwhelming complexity** of professional terminals (TradingView, Bloomberg Terminal, E*TRADE Pro) — steep learning curves deter newcomers
2. **Lack of education-integrated tools** — most platforms separate learning from doing; this product embeds trading guides, risk calculators, and simulator directly into the dashboard
3. **No unified free platform** that combines multi-asset data (crypto, stocks, forex), technical indicators, sentiment analysis, paper trading, and portfolio tracking in a single interface

## Design Philosophy
> "The terminal your first $10K deserved."

- **Professional-grade** visual design: dark slate palette, monospace financial figures, dense information density, subtle borders and elevation
- **Educational guardrails**: embedded per-asset trading guides, hardcoded 1% risk rule, minimum R:R enforcement, circuit breakers
- **Zero configuration to start**: batteries-included symbol set, pre-configured indicators, sensible defaults for everything
- **Fully transparent**: no black-box signals, all indicators documented, all risk parameters visible and configurable

## Non-Goals (Explicit)
- Not a registered broker — no real-money execution
- Not a copy-trading or social platform
- Not a mobile app — desktop-first, tablet-tolerant
- Not a portfolio rebalancing tool
- Not a direct competitor to TradingView (different scope: analysis + paper trading vs. charting + community)
